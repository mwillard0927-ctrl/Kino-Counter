    How to use it

    Starting with the $1 bills, type how many are in the drawer on the big keypad, then tap Next to move up one bill at a time until you finish the $100s.

    The Drawer total at the top adds up as you go, and you can tap any small bill in the bill strip to jump back and fix a count.

    When you're done, you'll see every bill with its count and value in big print, plus the Grand total and number of bills — tap Save to history to keep it.

    The History tab lists each saved audit with its date and time so you can open, fix or delete it, and the QR code lets a coworker scan and open the app on their own phone.
